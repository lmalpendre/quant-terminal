    How to read this

    Forecasts come from simple statistical models (trend extrapolation, ridge regression on technical features, and a random-walk baseline) fitted to past daily prices. The ranges show how much prices have typically moved, not guarantees. The walk-forward backtest tells you honestly whether a model has beaten "no change" on this asset in the past two years. Markets are driven by news no model can see. This is an educational tool, not financial advice.

    Data: Yahoo Finance / Stooq daily closes (via data/market.json), live crypto from Binance or CoinGecko, live metal spot from gold-api.com. Copper is the COMEX front-month future in USD per pound.
